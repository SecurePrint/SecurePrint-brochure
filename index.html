The Process

How SecurePrint Works
from Day One

From a student's perspective, using SecurePrint takes under two minutes. From your institution's perspective, it requires zero daily intervention.

1

Upload & Configure

The user visits printeriitb.shop, uploads their PDF document, selects the printer location on your campus, and configures settings — page count, copies, colour mode, duplex — from any device, anywhere.

2

Review & Pay

The platform calculates the total based on the location's pricing. The user pays via Razorpay (UPI, card, netbanking) or, at Institute Pay-enabled printers, authenticates with their institutional ID and OTP.

3

Automated Print Dispatch

Upon payment confirmation, the print job is dispatched automatically to the selected printer. No human intervention. No queue management. The printer executes the job and the document is ready for collection.

4

Collect & Done

The user collects their printout from the printer location. For Institute Pay users, the transaction is logged against their ID and included in the institution's next monthly invoice — no further action required from the user or institution.
